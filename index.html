Technical Details & FAQs

I am an undergraduate student in Informatics (Computer Science) at Universitas Sebelas Maret (UNS) in Surakarta, Indonesia. My engineering focus centers on high-throughput backend infrastructure, reactive client-side applications, and applied mathematical computing.
The application translates university course schedules into temporal matrix intervals. It detects overlaps in real-time, highlights conflicting periods, and allows students to export conflict-free timetables into high-resolution PNGs or calendar iCal (.ics) formats. You can try the live demo at managery-schedule-uns.vercel.app.
Project-EigenFace computes the covariance matrix from centered facial image vectors and calculates its orthonormal Eigenvectors via Principal Component Analysis (PCA). The top-k principal components capture over 94% of dataset variance for low-dimensional Euclidean distance classification.
AgroAI operates as an asynchronous FastAPI backend service containerized with Docker. It queries PostgreSQL for soil and altitude profiling data, computes dynamic N-P-K nutrient schedules, and prompts Groq LLM / Google GenAI SDK for contextual agricultural recommendations.
